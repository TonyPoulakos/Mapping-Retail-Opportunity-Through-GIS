# Mapping Retail Opportunity Through GIS

**Exploring how GIS and location intelligence can support retail location planning, network strategy, and market expansion.**

Mapping Retail Opportunity Through GIS is an independent portfolio series examining how spatial analysis can help identify, evaluate, and communicate retail location opportunities.

Each project explores a different location-planning question by bringing together geographic, demographic, market, accessibility, development, and retail-network factors within a GIS-based analytical framework.

## Featured Analyses

The series explores different retail markets, brands, and location-planning challenges, including:

- **LEGO | Mumbai, India** — Evaluating potential locations for retail market entry
- **UNIQLO | Dallas–Fort Worth, Texas** — Examining geographic opportunity for continued market expansion
- **ALDI | Phoenix, Arizona** — Identifying emerging opportunities within a growing retail network
- **New Balance | Boston, Massachusetts** — Exploring retail location strategy through an interactive GIS-based analysis

Additional analyses will be added as the series continues.

## Analytical Approach

Depending on the market and business question, projects may incorporate:

- Demographic and population patterns
- Existing store and competitive networks
- Trade areas and market accessibility
- Transportation and connectivity
- Population and development growth
- Retail concentrations and market structure
- Site and market characteristics
- Spatial modeling and GIS visualization

The objective is not simply to map these factors individually, but to bring them together to better understand how geography can influence retail planning and network decisions.
