About the Series

Different markets require different questions.

Mapping Retail Opportunity Through GIS examines how spatial analytics can help frame and evaluate different retail location decisions across markets, brands, and stages of network development.

Each analysis applies a different strategic lens, demonstrating that retail opportunity is not defined by one universal site-selection formula. Market potential, network whitespace, accessibility, consumer characteristics, competitive context, distribution presence, and brand fit can all shape the answer depending on the business question.
